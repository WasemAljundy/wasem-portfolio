# Wasem Aljundy — Portfolio Product Blueprint v1

## 1. Product Goal
Build a premium engineering portfolio that positions Wasem Aljundy as a Senior Flutter Engineer capable of owning mobile products end-to-end: architecture, implementation, integrations, quality, deployment, and iteration.

The site must serve two audiences simultaneously:
- Recruiters / Hiring Managers: understand value, seniority, breadth, availability, and contact path quickly.
- CTOs / Tech Leads / Senior Engineers: see evidence of technical depth, architecture thinking, real production work, and product ownership.

## 2. Core Positioning
Primary title:
**Senior Flutter Engineer**

Supporting line:
**Building production-grade mobile products from architecture to release.**

Supporting proof points:
- 4+ years of Flutter / mobile engineering experience
- 20+ production applications
- Flutter + Dart + Android
- Clean Architecture and modern state management
- Firebase, REST APIs, payments, maps, notifications, health integrations
- App Store and Google Play delivery
- AI-assisted engineering workflows

## 3. Design Direction
Apple-inspired, not Apple-cloned.

Principles:
- restrained visual system
- strong typography hierarchy
- generous whitespace
- cinematic project presentation
- fluid but purposeful motion
- premium device mockups
- subtle depth/materials
- responsive interactions
- reduced-motion support
- very fast perceived performance

Avoid:
- template-like portfolio cards
- excessive gradients
- decorative animation without purpose
- skill-logo walls
- giant paragraphs
- fake metrics
- unsupported claims

## 7. Visual Asset Strategy
Use the local MyWorks archive as the primary visual source.

Priority order:
1. existing polished mockups / marketing compositions
2. strongest raw screenshots grouped into product journeys
3. official App Store / Google Play imagery for live products when local assets are missing
4. custom device compositions generated from real screenshots during implementation

Do not display screenshots as a generic grid on the homepage.
Use them as cinematic product storytelling elements.

## 10. Content Rules
- Site language: English first
- Keep Arabic project names when they are part of the product identity
- Avoid generic AI wording
- Avoid self-rating skill percentages
- Avoid fake progress bars
- Avoid excessive buzzwords
- Every claim should be evidence-backed
- Every featured project must answer: what was built, why it was difficult, what Wasem owned, and what exists today
